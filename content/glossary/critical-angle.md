# Critical angle

The incidence angle, in the higher-index material, for which the refracted ray would run along the interface. Its sine is the lower index divided by the higher index.

**Concrete example:** For core 1.50 and cladding 1.47, the critical angle is about 78.5 degrees, measured from the boundary normal.

**Keep the distinction:** It is not the external acceptance angle of a fibre.
