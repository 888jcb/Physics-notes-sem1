# Dispersion

Dependence of wave propagation on frequency or, in multimode fibres, differences in mode delays. In communications this can broaden a pulse in time.

**Concrete example:** A short input flash can arrive as a wider output pulse even when enough optical power remains.

**Keep the distinction:** In a material, dispersion usually refers specifically to frequency dependence; modal dispersion concerns different guided modes.
