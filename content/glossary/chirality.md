# Chirality

For a nanotube, the way the graphene lattice wraps around its circumference, labelled by a pair of integers. This wrapping changes allowed electronic states.

**Concrete example:** Different wrapping patterns can help determine whether a single-walled CNT is metallic or semiconducting.

**Keep the distinction:** It is not simply which direction the whole tube points in a drawing; diameter and structural details also matter.
