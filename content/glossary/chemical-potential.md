# Chemical potential

The thermodynamic energy change associated with adding a particle, with the appropriate other variables held fixed. In occupation functions it sets the energy reference for filling states.

**Concrete example:** At finite temperature, the Fermi-Dirac occupation is one half at energy E equal to chemical potential mu.

**Keep the distinction:** Chemical does not mean it applies only to chemical reactions. Mu can vary with temperature.
