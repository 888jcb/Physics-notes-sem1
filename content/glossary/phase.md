# Phase

The position within a wave's repeating cycle, usually measured as an angle. A phase difference tells us whether two waves' peaks arrive together or at different parts of the cycle.

**Concrete example:** Two equal waves with coincident peaks can reinforce; a half-cycle phase difference can cancel them.

**Keep the distinction:** Phase is not amplitude. Two equally bright waves can have different phases.
