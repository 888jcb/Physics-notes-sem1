# Bragg angle

The angle theta between an incident X-ray beam and the crystal planes in Bragg's law.

**Concrete example:** If a diffractometer reports 2 theta = 40 degrees, insert theta = 20 degrees into 2d sin(theta) = n lambda.

**Keep the distinction:** It is measured from the plane, whereas optical incidence angles are commonly measured from the normal.
