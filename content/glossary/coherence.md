# Coherence

A sufficiently stable phase relationship between waves over the times and distances relevant to an experiment. It allows a stable interference pattern to be recorded.

**Concrete example:** A laser split into object and reference beams can provide the mutual coherence needed for a hologram.

**Keep the distinction:** Coherence is not simply brightness, and it has finite spatial and temporal ranges.
