# Total internal reflection

Confinement of light at an ideal boundary when it approaches from the higher-index side at an incidence angle greater than the critical angle. No propagating transmitted ray carries energy away into the second medium in this ideal model.

**Concrete example:** Suitable rays in a fibre repeatedly meet the core boundary at a sufficiently large angle.

**Keep the distinction:** A decaying evanescent field still extends into the cladding; total reflection does not mean zero field beyond the boundary.
