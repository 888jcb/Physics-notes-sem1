# Allotrope

A distinct structural form of the same element, arising from different atomic arrangements and bonding.

**Concrete example:** Diamond and graphite both contain carbon but have different networks and very different electrical and mechanical behaviour.

**Keep the distinction:** They are different structures of one element, not different chemical elements.
