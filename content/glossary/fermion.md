# Fermion

An identical quantum-particle species with half-integer spin and antisymmetric exchange behaviour. No two identical fermions occupy the same complete single-particle state.

**Concrete example:** Two electrons can share one spatial orbital if their spin states differ.

**Keep the distinction:** The exclusion rule forbids identical complete states, not merely equal energies or nearby positions.
