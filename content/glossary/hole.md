# Hole

An unoccupied valence-band electron state whose collective motion can be described as a positive charge carrier. Neighbouring electrons filling vacancies make the vacancy appear to move.

**Concrete example:** If electrons shift left into successive vacancies, the vacancy moves right.

**Keep the distinction:** A hole is not a proton and is not the same as a stationary positively charged dopant ion.
