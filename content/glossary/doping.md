# Doping

Controlled introduction of impurity atoms to alter semiconductor carrier concentrations. Donors supply electrons; acceptors enable holes.

**Concrete example:** Adding a small concentration of phosphorus to silicon can make it n-type.

**Keep the distinction:** The purpose is controlled electrical behaviour, not uncontrolled contamination or net charging of the whole specimen.
