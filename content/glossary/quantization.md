# Quantization

The appearance of discrete allowed values in a specified physical system. Boundary conditions and the system's quantum dynamics determine what is discrete.

**Concrete example:** Bound atomic electrons have discrete energy levels; emitted photons correspond to differences between those levels.

**Keep the distinction:** Not every energy in nature must be an integer multiple of one universal energy unit.
