# Miller indices

Miller indices specify crystal-plane orientation using reciprocals of axis intercepts in lattice units. A zero means parallel to that axis.

**Concrete example:** Intercepts a, 2b and infinity c give reciprocals 1, one half and 0, hence orientation (210).

**Keep the distinction:** Round brackets label planes; square brackets label directions. Plane orientation does not specify its unique absolute position.
