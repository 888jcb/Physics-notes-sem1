# Wavefront

A surface joining points of equal wave phase at a given instant. Its shape describes how the wave is spreading.

**Concrete example:** A distant source can produce approximately planar wavefronts; a point source produces approximately spherical ones.

**Keep the distinction:** A wavefront is not a single ray. Rays indicate propagation directions in the ray approximation.
