# Pauli exclusion principle

Identical fermions cannot occupy the same complete single-particle quantum state.

**Concrete example:** Opposite-spin electrons can occupy the same spatial orbital because their complete states are different.

**Keep the distinction:** It does not limit an entire energy level to one electron if that level contains several states.
