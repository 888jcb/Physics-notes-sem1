# Energy band

A range of closely spaced allowed electron energies in a solid. Interactions among many atoms produce a very large number of allowed states.

**Concrete example:** A semiconductor has a valence band and a higher conduction band separated by a gap.

**Keep the distinction:** A band is a range of allowed energies, not a physical strip inside the specimen.
