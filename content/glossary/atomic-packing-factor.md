# Atomic packing factor

The fraction of unit-cell volume filled by equal hard-sphere atoms assigned to the cell. It is a geometric model, not a literal measurement of sharp electron-cloud boundaries.

**Concrete example:** FCC has APF about 0.740, meaning about 74 percent of the model cell volume is occupied.

**Keep the distinction:** More atoms per cell alone does not establish denser packing; cell size and atomic radius matter too.
