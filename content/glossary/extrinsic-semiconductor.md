# Extrinsic semiconductor

A semiconductor whose carriers are substantially controlled by intentional doping. It can be n-type or p-type.

**Concrete example:** Donor-doped silicon has electrons as majority carriers under usual extrinsic conditions.

**Keep the distinction:** Minority carriers still exist; doping does not eliminate the opposite carrier type.
