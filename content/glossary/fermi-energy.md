# Fermi energy

The highest occupied single-particle energy at absolute zero for the usual noninteracting electron filling picture, equivalent to the zero-temperature chemical potential in the standard bulk limit.

**Concrete example:** At zero temperature, states below it are filled and states above it empty.

**Keep the distinction:** At nonzero temperature there is a distribution of occupations rather than a sharp highest filled boundary.
