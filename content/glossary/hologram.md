# Hologram

A hologram is a recorded interference structure made from object and reference waves. Suitable illumination diffracts from that structure and reconstructs an object-like wavefront.

**Concrete example:** Moving your head can reveal a different perspective in a reconstructed holographic image.

**Keep the distinction:** It records phase information indirectly through intensity fringes; ordinary photographic film does not directly measure phase.
