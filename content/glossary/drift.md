# Drift

Average directed carrier motion caused by an electric field, superposed on random motion.

**Concrete example:** Electron drift is opposite the electric field; hole drift is along the field.

**Keep the distinction:** In a junction at equilibrium, drift and diffusion currents balance even though particles keep moving.
