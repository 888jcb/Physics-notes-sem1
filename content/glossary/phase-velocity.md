# Phase velocity

The speed of a constant-phase point of a wave, such as a crest: vp = omega/k.

**Concrete example:** For a nonrelativistic free matter wave using kinetic energy, vp is half the particle speed.

**Keep the distinction:** Phase speed need not be the speed of information or of a localized particle. State the energy convention.
