# Microstate

One detailed microscopic configuration in the model: particle assignments for distinguishable particles, or suitable occupation numbers for quantum particles.

**Concrete example:** AB on the left and C on the right is one configuration; AC on the left and B on the right is another.

**Keep the distinction:** The description depends on particle identity and constraints. Do not label indistinguishable particles as distinguishable without justification.
