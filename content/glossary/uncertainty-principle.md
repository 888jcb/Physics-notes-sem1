# Uncertainty principle

The quantum limit on simultaneous spreads of a position coordinate and its conjugate momentum in a state: delta x delta px is at least hbar/2.

**Concrete example:** Confining an electron more tightly requires a broader range of momentum components.

**Keep the distinction:** It is not merely carelessness in measurement and does not say every pair of physical quantities has this same bound.
