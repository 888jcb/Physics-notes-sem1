# Refraction

A change in propagation direction when a wave crosses into a medium with a different phase speed at an oblique angle. Frequency stays the same at a stationary interface; wavelength changes.

**Concrete example:** A ray entering glass from air bends towards the normal when the glass has the higher index.

**Keep the distinction:** At normal incidence its speed can change without its direction changing.
