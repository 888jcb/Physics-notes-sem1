# Entropy

A thermodynamic state quantity related, in the equal-weight microcanonical model, to microscopic multiplicity by S = kB ln W.

**Concrete example:** A macrostate compatible with more microstates has larger entropy under those assumptions.

**Keep the distinction:** Disorder is only a loose memory aid; counting accessible states is the more precise explanation here.
