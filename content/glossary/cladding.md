# Cladding

The lower-index optical material surrounding a fibre core. It provides a controlled guiding boundary and isolates it from direct changes in the surroundings.

**Concrete example:** Core index 1.50 and cladding index 1.47 can guide suitable rays by total internal reflection.

**Keep the distinction:** It is an optical part of the fibre, not merely mechanical packaging.
