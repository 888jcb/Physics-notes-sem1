# Interference

The change in resultant intensity when overlapping wave amplitudes add. Relative phase controls whether the result reinforces or cancels.

**Concrete example:** Two coherent beams produce bright and dark fringes where their path differences give different relative phases.

**Keep the distinction:** For coherent waves, add amplitudes first and calculate intensity afterwards; adding intensities alone loses the fringes.
