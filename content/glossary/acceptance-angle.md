# Acceptance angle

The maximum launch half-angle to the fibre axis accepted for guiding in the ideal meridional-ray model. Larger launch angles fail the guiding condition at the core boundary.

**Concrete example:** In air, NA = 0.30 gives a half-angle of about 17.5 degrees; the full cone is about 35 degrees.

**Keep the distinction:** Measure it outside the entrance face, not from the normal to the side wall.
