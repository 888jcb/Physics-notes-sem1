# Numerical aperture

A dimensionless measure of a fibre's ability to accept incident light. In the ideal step-index ray model, NA = n0 sin(theta_a) = sqrt(n1 squared minus n2 squared).

**Concrete example:** With a fixed core and cladding, putting the entrance in water reduces the accepted external angle even though the model NA stays unchanged.

**Keep the distinction:** NA is not itself an angle. Use the external-medium index when converting it to an angle.
