# de Broglie wavelength

The wavelength associated with a particle's momentum: lambda = h/p. It predicts matter-wave diffraction.

**Concrete example:** An electron accelerated from rest through 100 V has a nonrelativistic wavelength about 0.123 nm.

**Keep the distinction:** It does not describe a physical ripple drawn on the surface of an electron.
