# Quantum wire

A structure confining carriers in two transverse directions and allowing extended motion along its axis.

**Concrete example:** A sufficiently narrow conducting channel can act as a one-dimensional electronic system.

**Keep the distinction:** Not every ordinary thin metal wire has strong quantum confinement under all operating conditions.
