# Thermodynamic probability

The count W of microstates compatible with a specified macrostate. It is a nonnegative integer count, not a normalized probability.

**Concrete example:** Three compatible assignments mean W = 3. Their probability is 3/8 if all eight assignments in the model are equally likely.

**Keep the distinction:** Turning W into probability requires the total accessible possibilities and assumptions about their weights.
