# Normalization

Choosing the overall amplitude of a state so its total probability over the allowed space is one.

**Concrete example:** If the integral of absolute psi squared is 4, divide psi by 2, not by 4.

**Keep the distinction:** Normalization does not mean setting psi equal to one everywhere.
