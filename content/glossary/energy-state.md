# Energy state

A specific allowed quantum state, distinguished by the full relevant quantum labels. Several distinct states can share one energy value.

**Concrete example:** Electrons with opposite spin in the same spatial orbital occupy different complete single-particle states.

**Keep the distinction:** An energy level specifies an energy value; it need not identify a unique state.
