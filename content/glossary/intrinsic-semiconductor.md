# Intrinsic semiconductor

An ideally pure semiconductor in which conduction electrons and holes are generated in pairs. At equilibrium their number densities are equal: n = p = ni.

**Concrete example:** Heating intrinsic silicon increases the population of thermally generated electron-hole pairs.

**Keep the distinction:** Pure does not mean it has no carriers at every temperature.
