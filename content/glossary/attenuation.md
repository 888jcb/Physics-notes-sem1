# Attenuation

Loss of optical power as light travels through a system. Absorption, scattering, bends and imperfect joints can contribute.

**Concrete example:** A 3 dB power loss leaves approximately half the input power; 10 dB leaves one tenth.

**Keep the distinction:** Attenuation weakens a pulse. Dispersion spreads its arrival times.
