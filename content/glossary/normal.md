# Normal

An imaginary line perpendicular to a surface at the point where a ray meets it. In reflection and Snell's law, angles are measured from this line.

**Concrete example:** For a horizontal surface, the normal is vertical. A ray parallel to the surface makes 90 degrees with its normal.

**Keep the distinction:** The normal changes when the surface orientation changes; the fibre axis is not every surface's normal.
