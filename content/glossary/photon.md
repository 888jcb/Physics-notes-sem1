# Photon

A quantum of the electromagnetic field. A photon of frequency f carries energy hf and vacuum momentum h/lambda.

**Concrete example:** Blue light has more energy per photon than red light because it has a higher frequency.

**Keep the distinction:** A photon is not a tiny classical bead carrying a miniature sinusoidal wave inside it.
