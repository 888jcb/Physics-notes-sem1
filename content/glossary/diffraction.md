# Diffraction

Wave spreading and interference associated with openings, obstacles or spatially varying structures. It becomes conspicuous when structural length scales are comparable to wavelength.

**Concrete example:** A crystal diffracts X-rays, and a hologram diffracts reconstruction light.

**Keep the distinction:** The phenomena share wave physics, but a diffraction pattern is not always a simple mirror reflection.
