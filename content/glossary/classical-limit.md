# Classical limit

A regime in which quantum exchange effects are negligible, so BE and FD occupations approach Maxwell-Boltzmann behaviour.

**Concrete example:** When exp((E-mu)/kBT) is much larger than one, the extra minus or plus one in the denominator becomes negligible.

**Keep the distinction:** High temperature alone is not sufficient at every density; low occupation of individual states is the relevant condition.
