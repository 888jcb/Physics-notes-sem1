# Blackbody

An ideal absorber of all incident electromagnetic radiation whose equilibrium emission spectrum depends on temperature.

**Concrete example:** A small hole in a heated cavity approximates a blackbody because entering light is very unlikely to escape.

**Keep the distinction:** A blackbody can glow brightly when hot. Black refers to ideal absorption, not permanent visual darkness.
