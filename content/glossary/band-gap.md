# Band gap

The energy interval between valence-band maximum and conduction-band minimum without allowed bulk electronic states in the ideal semiconductor band model.

**Concrete example:** An electron must gain sufficient energy to move from the valence band into the conduction band.

**Keep the distinction:** The gap is an energy interval, not an empty physical hole in the crystal. Defect states can occur inside a real material's gap.
