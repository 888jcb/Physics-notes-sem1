# Diffusion

Net spreading due to random motion when concentration varies across space. More particles cross from the more populated side than the less populated side.

**Concrete example:** Electrons initially diffuse from an n region into a neighbouring p region.

**Keep the distinction:** Random individual motion can produce a directed net flow without an applied electric field.
