# Core

The central light-guiding region of an optical fibre. In the introductory fibre model its refractive index is greater than that of the surrounding cladding.

**Concrete example:** The core is the central region shown between two cladding layers in the ray diagram.

**Keep the distinction:** The protective plastic coating is not the core or the optical cladding.
