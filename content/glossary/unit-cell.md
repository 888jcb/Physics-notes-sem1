# Unit cell

A volume which can be repeated by translation to build a periodic lattice or crystal. Boundary atoms are shared with neighbouring cells.

**Concrete example:** Eight corner atoms contribute one atom in total to a simple-cubic conventional cell.

**Keep the distinction:** A drawn corner atom is not owned entirely by one cell.
