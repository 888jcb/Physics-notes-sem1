# Quantum confinement

Restriction of carrier wave states by small dimensions and potential barriers, producing size-dependent allowed energies.

**Concrete example:** A sufficiently thin semiconductor layer confines motion across its thickness while permitting motion along the layer.

**Keep the distinction:** Small size alone does not guarantee strong confinement; material length scales and barrier properties matter.
