# Carbon nanotube

A cylindrical carbon network structurally related to a rolled graphene sheet. Single-walled and nested multi-walled versions exist.

**Concrete example:** Some tubes can act as semiconducting channels; others provide metallic conduction paths.

**Keep the distinction:** Not all nanotubes are metallic. Rolled graphene describes geometry, not every manufacturing process.
