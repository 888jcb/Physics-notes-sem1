# Graphene

A single layer of carbon atoms in a connected honeycomb lattice. Strong in-plane bonds and electronic structure give useful mechanical and transport properties.

**Concrete example:** It can be used in conducting films, sensors and composite research.

**Keep the distinction:** Graphite is a stack of such layers; ideal monolayer graphene also lacks an ordinary semiconductor band gap in the simplest model.
