# Effective mass

A parameter describing how a carrier responds dynamically within a material's band structure. It includes effects of the periodic crystal environment in a simplified model.

**Concrete example:** Semiconductor confinement formulas often use an electron or hole effective mass instead of the free-electron mass.

**Keep the distinction:** It does not mean the electron's fundamental rest mass has physically changed.
