# Threshold frequency

The lowest light frequency that can cause photoemission in the simple one-photon model. It equals the work function divided by Planck's constant.

**Concrete example:** Below threshold, increasing ordinary light intensity adds more individually insufficient photons.

**Keep the distinction:** This statement assumes the ordinary one-photon regime, not intense multiphoton processes.
