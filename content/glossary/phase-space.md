# Phase space

A mathematical space of positions and momenta. One point specifies all of these coordinates for a classical system.

**Concrete example:** One particle moving on a line needs x and p. N particles in three dimensions need 6N coordinates before constraints are imposed.

**Keep the distinction:** This is not ordinary physical space or a plot of wave phase.
