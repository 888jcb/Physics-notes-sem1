# Fermi level

The electron chemical potential at the stated temperature. It sets the balance of occupied and unoccupied states in the Fermi-Dirac distribution.

**Concrete example:** In a semiconductor the Fermi level can lie in the band gap, even though no allowed bulk electron state exists there in the ideal picture.

**Keep the distinction:** Strictly, Fermi energy is the zero-temperature value; introductory notes sometimes use EF for both.
