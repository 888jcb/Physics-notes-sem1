# Mode

An allowed field pattern that satisfies the wave equation and the structure's boundary conditions. A guided mode has its own spatial pattern and propagation behaviour.

**Concrete example:** A single-mode fibre supports one guided spatial mode at its operating wavelength, with polarization details treated separately.

**Keep the distinction:** A mode is not a colour, photon, or individual zigzag ray.
