# Work function

The minimum energy needed to remove an electron from a material into vacuum under the specified surface conditions.

**Concrete example:** A photon of 3 eV incident on a surface with work function 2 eV can leave at most 1 eV as emitted-electron kinetic energy in the simple model.

**Keep the distinction:** It is not a voltage. Convert electronvolt energy to stopping voltage using the electron charge.
