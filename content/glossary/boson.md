# Boson

An identical quantum-particle species with integer spin and symmetric exchange behaviour. Bosons may share a complete single-particle state.

**Concrete example:** Photons are bosons, and occupation of a photon mode can exceed one.

**Keep the distinction:** Bosons generally obey Bose-Einstein statistics; Maxwell-Boltzmann applies only in an appropriate dilute classical limit.
