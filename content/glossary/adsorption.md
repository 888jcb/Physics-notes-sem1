# Adsorption

Attachment of particles or molecules to a surface. More accessible surface can create more binding sites.

**Concrete example:** A suitably engineered adsorbent can capture selected pollutants on its surface.

**Keep the distinction:** Absorption means uptake into the bulk; adsorption means attachment at a surface. Selectivity and capacity depend on the material.
