# Depletion region

The junction region depleted mainly of mobile majority carriers, leaving fixed ionised donors and acceptors. Those ions create a built-in electric field.

**Concrete example:** Positive donor ions lie on the n side; negative acceptor ions lie on the p side. The field points from n to p.

**Keep the distinction:** Depleted does not mean empty of atoms, ions or all possible charge carriers.
