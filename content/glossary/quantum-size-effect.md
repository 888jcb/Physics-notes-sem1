# Quantum size effect

A size-dependent change in physical properties resulting from changes in quantum states, often caused by confinement.

**Concrete example:** Reducing the size of many semiconductor dots increases transition energies and shifts emission towards shorter wavelengths.

**Keep the distinction:** A larger surface-area-to-volume ratio is a separate mechanism; not every nanoscale effect is quantum.
