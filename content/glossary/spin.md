# Spin

An intrinsic quantum form of angular momentum, expressed in units involving hbar. Its allowed labels affect particle statistics and magnetic behaviour.

**Concrete example:** An electron has spin one half; a photon has spin one.

**Keep the distinction:** An electron is not a classical ball physically rotating about an axis to generate this property.
