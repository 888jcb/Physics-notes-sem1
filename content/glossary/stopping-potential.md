# Stopping potential

The reverse voltage magnitude needed to stop even the fastest emitted photoelectrons. It measures maximum kinetic energy through eVs = Kmax.

**Concrete example:** A maximum kinetic energy of 1.1 eV corresponds to a stopping-potential magnitude of 1.1 V.

**Keep the distinction:** It does not directly count how many photoelectrons are emitted.
