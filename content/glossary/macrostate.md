# Macrostate

A coarse description specifying bulk constraints or aggregate quantities without identifying every microscopic detail.

**Concrete example:** Two particles left and one right can describe several different distinguishable-particle microstates.

**Keep the distinction:** Several microstates can correspond to the same macrostate.
