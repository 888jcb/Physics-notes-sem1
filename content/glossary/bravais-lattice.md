# Bravais lattice

A periodic arrangement of equivalent points generated by translations. There are fourteen distinct types in three dimensions, grouped into seven crystal systems.

**Concrete example:** Simple, body-centred and face-centred cubic are the three cubic Bravais types.

**Keep the distinction:** The fourteen types classify translational geometry, not every possible atomic crystal structure.
