# Quantum dot

A structure confining carriers in all three directions, producing discrete atom-like levels and no extended free direction.

**Concrete example:** Semiconductor dots of different sizes can have different optical transition energies and emission colours.

**Keep the distinction:** Zero-dimensional does not mean zero volume or that a dot is one atom.
