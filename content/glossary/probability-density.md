# Probability density

Probability per unit coordinate interval or volume. The probability in a finite region is found by integration.

**Concrete example:** A density of 2 per metre over a uniform 0.1 m interval gives probability 0.2.

**Keep the distinction:** A density can exceed one in its units; an integrated probability cannot exceed one.
