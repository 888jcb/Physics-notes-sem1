# Group velocity

The propagation speed of the envelope of a narrow-band wave packet: vg = d omega/dk.

**Concrete example:** For a free massive particle, the group velocity equals its particle velocity under the usual dispersion relation.

**Keep the distinction:** In general dispersive media it cannot automatically be identified with signal speed.
