# Basis

The atom or group of atoms attached in the same way to every lattice point. Repeating the basis creates the crystal structure.

**Concrete example:** The same two-atom motif placed at every lattice point can make a crystal with two atoms in its basis.

**Keep the distinction:** A basis is not the same as a unit cell or a coordinate axis.
