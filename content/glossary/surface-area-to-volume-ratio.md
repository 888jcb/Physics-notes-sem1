# Surface-area-to-volume ratio

Exposed area divided by material volume, measured in inverse length. Smaller similar particles have a larger ratio.

**Concrete example:** For a sphere it is 3/r; halving radius doubles the ratio. More surface can supply more accessible adsorption or reaction sites.

**Keep the distinction:** Aggregation can hide surfaces. Count accessible area when explaining an application.
