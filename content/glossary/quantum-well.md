# Quantum well

A structure confining carriers in one direction while permitting extended motion in two. Its electronic motion is called two-dimensional.

**Concrete example:** A thin low-potential layer between barrier materials can provide confined transverse states and free in-plane motion.

**Keep the distinction:** Its physical thickness is finite. Two-dimensional counts free directions, not confined directions.
